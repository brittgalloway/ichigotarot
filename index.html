Our Story

Ichigo Tarot is a luxury publishing and game company. We chose tarot as our focus for its ability to combine fine art and meaning in everyday life. Both art collectors and serious diviners will see how much symbolism and care are put into each and every card.

Our deluxe packages include a full 78-card deck, an oracle deck, a guidebook, a journal, and one special item unique to each theme. We pride ourselves on creating high-level art and unique experiences.
